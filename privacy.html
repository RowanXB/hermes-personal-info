Hermes Personal Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who operates this integration

Hermes Personal is operated by Chengyan Tong for personal use with their own Google account. Questions about this integration or its handling of data can be sent to chengyantong3@gmail.com.

Google data accessed and purposes

Subject to the Google OAuth permissions granted and the tasks requested, Hermes may access and process Gmail message content, metadata and attachments; Calendar events and participants; Drive file content and metadata; Docs documents; Sheets spreadsheet content; and Contacts information. It uses this information to carry out the owner’s requested assistant tasks, including search, summaries, drafting, and authorised changes to Google Workspace data. Listing a service here does not grant additional OAuth permissions.

Model providers and other recipients

Information retrieved from Google, including message or document content and personal information contained in it, may be included in prompts, tool results or other context sent to the model provider and any API gateway configured in Hermes. Those services process data under their own terms and the owner’s account settings. Provider retention, logging and data-use practices depend on the chosen service and configuration; this policy does not promise that processing remains local or that providers retain no data.

Google receives the API requests needed to perform the task. If the owner instructs Hermes to send information to another person or service, the selected information may also be disclosed to that recipient.

Storage and retention

OAuth authorisation information is handled by the owner’s local integration. Retrieved content and generated outputs may persist in Hermes conversation history, memory, logs, cache, exported files or backups, depending on the installation and settings. Data sent to configured providers may also be retained by those providers. There is no single retention period asserted for all these locations.

Control, revocation and deletion

The owner can stop using the integration and revoke its Google access through their Google Account’s third-party connections settings. Revoking access stops future authorised API access but does not automatically erase previously saved conversations, files, logs, memories, backups or provider-held copies. These must be managed separately through the relevant local storage or provider controls. Contact the operator for questions about stored copies.

This information website

These static pages contain no login form, analytics script or Google data collection code. The web hosting service may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser metadata, under its own privacy practices. Google account content and OAuth credentials are not published on this website.

Changes

This policy should be updated when the integration’s data handling changes.
